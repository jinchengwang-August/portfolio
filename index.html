The University of California was founded in 1868 with ten faculty and forty students. In 1873 it moved to Berkeley beside Strawberry Creek. The Daily Californian traces its roots to early student publications and became a daily paper in 1897. In 1964, students at Sproul Plaza challenged restrictions on political advocacy and created a movement whose cultural influence traveled far beyond campus. This is not nostalgia as decoration. It is a working archive: a record of people questioning inherited systems, building new ones, and leaving room for the next voice. Research, products, music, movement, and ordinary curiosity occupy the same edition.
